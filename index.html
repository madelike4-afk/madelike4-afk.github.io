Shorts Pipeline

Shorts Pipeline is a private publishing tool for the YouTube channel Made Like This, which makes short videos about how industrial machines and processes work.

What it does

The tool runs on the channel owner's own computer. It turns a video clip into a finished Short with a script, an AI-generated voiceover and captions. It then uploads that Short to the channel using the YouTube Data API.

Who it's for

Only the channel owner uses this tool. It isn't offered to the public, and it doesn't access anyone else's Google or YouTube account.
